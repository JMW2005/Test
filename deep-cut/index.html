The dig

Each round shows a prompt like "Name a breed of dog". You have 20 seconds to type one answer that fits. You only get one locked-in answer per prompt, but a guess that isn't on the list doesn't use it up, so keep trying until the clock runs out.

Going deeper

Every accepted answer has a popularity. Answers most people give score little. Answers few people think of score more. Your total is your depth in metres.

The Mother Lode

Every prompt hides one Mother Lode. It isn't always the rarest answer. It's a satisfying one that some players will actually find. Hit it for the biggest score.

Spelling

Typos of a letter or two are forgiven on longer words, and common alternative names work too. The game always shows which answer your guess matched.
