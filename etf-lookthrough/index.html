          Live prices

          Add a free price key to value your shares at current prices.

          Get a free key at finnhub.io: sign up, then copy the API key from your dashboard. The key stays on this device. Prices refresh when you open the app, at most every 5 minutes. Mutual funds and some foreign tickers have no price there; type their price in on the Positions page.

          Fund holdings data

          Built-in holdings are approximate top holdings for popular funds. Fund weights change daily, so for exact numbers paste the current holdings from the fund provider's website. Any ticker not listed here is treated as an individual stock unless you add its holdings. Tap a fund to see or edit its holdings; funds with your own data are outlined.

          Display

          Built-in fund weights are approximations of recent top holdings and are for exploring your exposure, not for trading or tax decisions. Your positions, prices and key are saved only on this device. Prices can be delayed and are for estimates only.

          Fund holdings

          Paste straight from a provider's holdings CSV or table. Each line needs a ticker and a weight; a company name in between is kept. Weights left over after the listed holdings show under “Not broken out”.
